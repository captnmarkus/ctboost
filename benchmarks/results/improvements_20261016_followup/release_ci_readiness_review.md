Do not globally mock `psutil.Process` or disable the real watchdog tests. The controller's genuine platform-affinity checks should retain their own explicit capability policy.

These are test portability/dependency repairs, not changes to numerical tolerances, solver success rules, prediction oracles, experiment decisions, or production semantics.
